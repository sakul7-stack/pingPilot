About status pages
  • A status page shows the current status and uptime of your monitors to the public.
  • You'll get a shareable link like {{ site_url }}/dashboard/public/status/<slug>/.
  • You can add a custom domain (e.g. status.yourdomain.com) and verify it with a DNS TXT record.
  • After creating, you'll be taken to the editor to pick monitors, theme, and layout.
